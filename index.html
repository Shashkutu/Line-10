Now boarding

Every video is a journey.

Line 10 turns the player into a metro line. Chapters become stations, the playhead is a train, the title clatters onto a split-flap departure board, and playback speed is the service you ride.

HTML

Built from Video.js v10 components. Chapters and captions are plain WebVTT files, and playback-id is a Mux playback ID (or use src for any video file). The source and every option are on GitHub.

How to ride

  1. Stations are chapters

    Each chapter of the video is a stop on the line. Hover the line to see the station sign with a preview frame.

  2. The train is the playhead

    Drag it, click anywhere on the line, or use the arrow keys.

  3. NOW
    Departure board

    Shows the current station, the next stop and how long until you get there.

  4. ]
    Next stop

    Jump to the next chapter with the button, or press [ and ].

  5. 1.5×
    Service is speed

    Slow, Night, Local, Limited, Express (1.5×) and Rapid (2×).

  6. KJL
    Keyboard and screen readers

    Space/K play, ←/J and →/L skip 5 s, [ ] stations, ↑↓ volume, M mute, < > speed, F fullscreen, C captions, I picture-in-picture, 0–9 jump, S for Settings on small screens, and at the end of the line N for the next line or R to ride again. Built on Video.js components, so every control is labelled and every menu closes with Esc.
